## 2. Paper Review & Discussion - 20%

Each team selects one paper from the curated multimodal machine learning reading list. Presentations should emphasize understanding and critical analysis rather than a section-by-section summary.

| Criterion | Weight within Component | Expectations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Understanding of Problem & Contribution | 25% | Clearly explains the research problem, motivation, and key contributions. |
| Technical Understanding | 25% | Accurately explains the model, multimodal representations, alignment/fusion strategy, training, and experiments. |
| Critical Analysis | 20% | Identifies strengths, limitations, assumptions, failure modes, and possible improvements. |
| Presentation Organization & Clarity | 15% | Well-structured slides, clear explanations, appropriate pacing, and effective visual communication. |
| Q&A and Discussion | 10% | Responds accurately to questions and leads a substantive technical discussion. |
| Team Participation | 5% | All team members demonstrate meaningful participation and understanding. |

18 min presentation + 6 min Q&A/discussion
Approximately 25 min per team including transition.
